Featured project

Production tracking app for a garment exporter

I turned the factory's spreadsheet-based tracking, from cutting to packing, into a web app built around each person's role. I gathered the requirements, designed it, built it and handled delivery myself.

Next.js · TypeScriptSupabase / PostgresRow-Level Security$0/month hosting
  • Tracks every order by PO, then style, then variant (gender, colour, size, print) through cutting, tie-dye, sewing, printing/embroidery, checking, ironing and packing
  • Five roles, from owner to the person in charge of each stage, each with access to exactly what they need. Saved numbers can only be corrected by the owner, and every change is logged
  • Tracks numbered cartons that mix styles and sizes, plus work sent to outside vendors with good, rejected and shortage counts on return
  • Analytics show output for today, this week and in total for each PO. Every tab exports to Excel
