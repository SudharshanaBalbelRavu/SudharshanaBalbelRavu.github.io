Featured project

Real-time production visibility for a garment exporter

The owner of an export factory couldn't see where his orders stood without chasing people and reconciling a spreadsheet. I worked with him to define what the business needed to know each day, then used AI to build an app that answers those questions live.

What it means for the business

  • One live view of every order's progress, so status no longer depends on chasing people
  • Losses caught at the stage they happen, while there's still time to protect the delivery date
  • Control over outsourced work: who has the goods, and what came back good, rejected or short
  • Numbers the owner can trust: only he can correct a figure, and every change is recorded
  • $0 running cost, and everything exports to Excel, which the team already uses
